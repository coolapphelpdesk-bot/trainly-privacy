Privacy Policy

Trainly · last updated October 28, 2026

1. Summary

Trainly (“the app”) is a public transport app for Android published by Cool App Helpdesk. It shows departures near you, live vehicle positions, trip plans and service alerts. This policy covers every country edition of Trainly (for example Trainly France, coolapp.trainly.france).

  • There is no account or sign-in, and we (the developer) never receive any data about you, where you are or where you travel. We have no server.
  • To show departures and plan trips, the app asks a public transit data service (Transitous) for them. That request contains the place you are looking at: your location if you chose “Use my location”, otherwise the station you picked. See section 5.
  • Location is optional. You can pick any station instead, and the app works the same.
  • Your saved places, saved trips and settings stay on your device.
  • The app includes Google AdMob to show one banner ad on its Home screen. Ads are switched off until 1 September 2027; until then the ads SDK is not started and receives nothing. See section 2.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK.

2. Data we collect

The developer collects nothing. The app sends no personal data, usage data, analytics or crash reports to the developer or to any service operated by the developer. The only third-party SDK that can collect data is Google AdMob, described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner ad on its Home screen, below the departures, served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Ads are switched off until 1 September 2027: before that date the SDK is never started, requests no ads and collects nothing. From that date, AdMob may collect the following to show and measure ads:

This data goes to Google, not to the developer, and is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services). The ads SDK never receives your device’s location from the app, your saved places or your trips. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Apart from ads, the only data that leaves your device is what the app needs to fetch transit information — a position or a search text — sent to the transit data service and map tile service described in section 5, which return the departures, routes and maps you see. Those services are not operated by the developer, and no name, account, advertising ID or device identifier is sent to them.

Crash and stability statistics that Android shares with Google Play under your device’s own settings (“Android vitals”) are aggregated and anonymous; they contain nothing about where you are or where you travel.

These services are run by third parties that the developer has no contract with; they handle requests under their own policies. They do not receive any name, account, advertising ID or device identifier from the app.

When you use “Share my ETA” or “Report issue”, the app opens the sharing or email app you choose with the text filled in; nothing is sent until you send it yourself. “See all data sources” opens the Transitous sources page in your browser.

Apart from Google AdMob (section 2), the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, social or cloud-storage SDK.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the developer collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are not directed at children.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer stores no data off your device. Remove a saved place or trip with its × button, and change Home’s place in Settings. To remove everything, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Trainly → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. You can turn off the app’s location access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Trainly → Permissions. The transit and map services in section 5 keep their own server logs under their own policies; the developer cannot reach or delete them.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. All network requests use HTTPS. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
